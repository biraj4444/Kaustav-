Who I am

I am Kaustav Khanikar. I build creative software across web development, UI/UX design and software engineering, and I do it end to end: the idea, the interface, the code, the deployment and the explanation.

How I work

I begin with a question about the person using the thing. For Expressive Send it was whether sending a message could feel like sending something to someone, rather than a bubble appearing on screen. The prototype, the case study and the written declaration followed from that one question.

Then I choose the simplest technology that fully solves it. Sometimes that is a static page on Cloudflare. Sometimes it is a Flask application with MongoDB, Google OAuth and Cloudinary. I would rather cut a feature than ship something that is fragile.

Finally I finish the parts that make a project trustworthy: legal pages, support, search readiness and clear writing about what it does and what it does not.
